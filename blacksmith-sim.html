Blacksmith Simulator

Gameplay

Description

In **Blacksmith Simulator**, you take on the role of a village blacksmith, crafting weapons to fulfil requests from the local villagers. Each order sends you on a journey that starts in the mine, where you'll gather the ores needed to create your weapons.

From mining ore and smelting ingots to forging the final weapon, every stage is brought to life through a series of mini-games. Your performance determines the quality of your craftsmanship and the final value of each item.

Manage incoming requests, gather and store valuable resources, and keep your workshop ready for the next order. Your fellow villagers are counting on you to provide them with the finest weapons!

My Role

Due to a signed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), I can only share certain details about my contributions to Blacksmith Simulator and cannot include direct screenshots of the project. However, I can describe the areas I worked on and the responsibilities I had throughout development.

Game Design & Gameplay

I participated in designing aspects of the game's core gameplay loop, contributing ideas to improve player progression and the crafting experience. One of my suggestions was introducing a weapon quality system, where the player's performance in the crafting mini-games directly influences the quality and final value of the weapons they create.

UI, Mini-Games & Visual Feedback

I worked on UI elements across the game's different mini-games, making sure players had the information and feedback needed to understand their performance. I also adjusted mini-game flows and performance thresholds, while implementing additional visual feedback through UI elements and visual effects (VFX).

Testing & Bug Fixing

For three months during development, I was responsible for testing and fixing bugs across the game. This involved reviewing, testing, and debugging Scrum tasks submitted by different members of the team, helping identify issues and ensure that implemented features worked as intended.

Audio Implementation

I implemented sound effects throughout various parts of the game, adding audio feedback to interactions and gameplay events. I also introduced random pitch variation to selected sound effects, helping make repeated actions sound less repetitive and more natural.

Demo & Trailer Production

I helped prepare a dedicated scene for the game's demo version and contributed to the camera angle system used during trailer production. I also assisted with recording gameplay samples and editing one of the trailer versions.

Community Management

Alongside development, I helped maintain the game's Discord community, supporting communication with players and helping keep the community active throughout development.
